Kalshi mentions markets are a unique type of prediction market where traders bet on whether a specific public figure will be mentioned during official events like press briefings, interviews, or congressional hearings.

## How Do They Work?

Each mentions market is tied to a specific person and a specific event or time period. For example, a market might ask: *"Will the President mention 'tariffs' during tomorrow's press conference?"*

Traders can buy "Yes" or "No" shares based on their prediction. When the event occurs and transcripts are published, the market resolves based on whether the mention actually happened.

## Why Track Mentions?

Tracking what public figures say — and how often they say it — provides valuable insights:

- **Policy signals**: Frequent mentions of a topic often precede policy action
- **Market intelligence**: Prediction market prices reflect collective wisdom about likely outcomes
- **Media analysis**: Understanding speaker patterns helps journalists and researchers
