Donald Trump:
Thank you very much. Today we're announcing the creation of the U.S. Strategic Critical Minerals Reserve, the first-ever stockpile of critical minerals for civilian use in times of emergency. We have the greatest experts on Earth behind me. We're joined by General Motors CEO Mary Barra, who's setting records, and Robert Friedland of Ivanhoe Mines. We also have Senators Marco Rubio, Bernie Moreno, and others from my administration. Today we're launching Project Vault to ensure American businesses are never harmed by shortages again. Just as we have a Strategic Petroleum Reserve, we're creating this for American industry. It involves $10 billion in Export-Import Bank financing and $2 billion in private sector financing. Over the past year, my administration has taken extraordinary steps to ensure we have all the critical minerals and rare earths we need, expediting federal permitting and signing major deals worldwide.

Project Vault is a uniquely American solution, bringing together capital markets, manufacturers, and miners. This is the largest EXIM Bank deal in history by more than double, and it will drive national security and manufacturing.

You've also created a club of nations starting with Australia, Japan, and others to allow for the trade of critical minerals.
